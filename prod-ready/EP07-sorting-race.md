## Short description
Anna and Lumi race to sort 12 beakers from shortest to tallest, from the same order with the same clock. Anna swaps neighbours pass after pass (bubble sort), and Lumi splits around a pivot (quick sort). Lumi wins in 28.5 s vs 59.0 s, and with a million items the gap grows to about 500 billion vs 25 million comparisons.
