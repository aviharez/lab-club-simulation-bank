# EP01: Same Jump, Different Planets

- File: EP01-same-jump-different-planets.mp4 · 40 s · 1080×1920
- Topic: gravity. The same jump speed gives very different heights on different worlds (h = v²/2g).
- Key numbers (from the report; all jumps at v = 3.133 m/s):
  - Moon (Miyu): 3.03 m high, 3.87 s in the air
  - Mars (Anna): 1.32 m, 1.69 s
  - Earth (Lumi): 0.50 m, 0.64 s
  - Jupiter (Zuzu): 0.20 m, 0.25 s

## Short description
Four friends do the exact same jump on four planets. On Earth Lumi rises 0.5 m, on Jupiter Zuzu barely leaves the floor (0.2 m), and on the Moon Miyu floats 3 m up and stays in the air for almost 4 seconds. Same push, different gravity.
