# EP05: Galton Board

- File: EP05-galton-board.mp4 · 44 s · 1080×1920
- Topic: the binomial distribution. Many 50/50 bounces add up to a bell curve.
- Key numbers (from the report; 1,000 balls, 12 rows of pegs, 13 bins, seeded):
  - Middle bin 6: 217 balls (theory 924/4096 = 22.6%, about 226).
  - Edge bin 0: 1 ball (theory 1 in 4,096, about 0.24).
  - Only 1 path leads to an edge bin; 924 different paths lead to the middle.

## Short description
1,000 balls bounce through 12 rows of pegs, going left or right 50/50 at each one. Anna bets on the far-left bin, Zuzu on the middle. The far-left bin catches 1 ball and the middle 217, because only one path leads to the edge while 924 lead to the centre. Together the bins draw a bell curve.
