# EP02: Feather vs Hammer

- File: EP02-feather-hammer.mp4 · 41 s · 1080×1920
- Topic: free fall. Without air, everything falls at the same rate, whatever its weight.
- Key numbers (from the report; 3 m drop, g = 9.81 m/s², simplified air drag):
  - In air: hammer 0.78 s, feather 7.53 s
  - In a vacuum: both 0.78 s, √(2h/g)

## Short description
A hammer and a feather drop from 3 m. In air the hammer lands in 0.78 s, while the feather is still drifting down for 7.5 s. Then the air is pumped out of the column and they land together, both at 0.78 s. Heavier isn't faster; air is what slows the feather. Apollo 15 astronaut David Scott did the same test on the Moon in 1971.
