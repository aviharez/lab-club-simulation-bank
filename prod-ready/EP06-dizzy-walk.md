## Short description
Anna spins Zuzu until she's dizzy, then Zuzu takes 100 random steps (north, east, south or west) while Lumi walks 100 steps straight. Lumi ends 100 tiles away and Zuzu only 7.2. With 50 more random walkers, the typical distance comes out at about √100 = 10: random steps mostly cancel out.
